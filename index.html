If the risk is HIGH

Move away from steep slopes, retaining walls, gullies and visibly damaged roads. Avoid crossing flowing water or recently failed ground. Follow official instructions from local authorities. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services.
SlopeWatch is a hackathon-ready early-warning prototype. Risk values are informational and are not an official warning, evacuation order, or substitute for local disaster-management authorities.
